# Zenote product context

## Overview

Zenote is a commercial AI chat SaaS: **Powerful AI, without the tight limits.** It gives people a polished, familiar place to chat with strong AI models, with multimodal input where a model supports it.

## Target user and value proposition

Zenote is for people in the Philippines who want capable AI chat for everyday work, study, writing, coding, and creative tasks without navigating several separate AI products. Its value is one clean chat experience, choice of models, generous but sustainable usage, PHP pricing, and locally practical payment options.

## Current positioning

Zenote should feel premium, calm, quick, and immediately understandable to existing ChatGPT users. It is a focused alternative for users who value strong models, a familiar interface, and Philippine-friendly commerce—not a general work platform.

## Core capabilities

- AI chat with conversation history and streaming responses.
- Multiple models through a single product-facing model catalogue.
- Image and file input where the selected model supports it.
- Markdown and code rendering.
- Authentication, plan-aware usage limits, and subscriptions.
- PHP pricing and payment methods practical for the Philippines where provider support is available.

## Product principles

Product decisions should favor:

- A dependable chat experience over feature volume.
- Clear model capability and usage information over vague promises.
- Financially safe, auditable limits over unlimited-use claims.
- Familiar interactions with deliberate visual polish.
- Provider flexibility without exposing provider complexity to users.

## Current differentiation

Zenote aims to differentiate through generous AI usage for its plans, Philippine-friendly payments, PHP pricing, a clean AI chat UX, and access to strong models through one product. Do not claim it is cheaper or more generous than a competitor unless current pricing and limits substantiate the claim.

## Commercial SaaS model

Zenote uses a Free / Plus / Pro subscription model. The tiers are a product concept, not a promise of final prices or limits:

- **Free:** an accessible entry point with controlled usage.
- **Plus:** more capacity and model access for regular individual use.
- **Pro:** the highest capacity and access appropriate for heavier individual use.

Pricing is displayed in Philippine pesos (PHP). Payment support should include GCash, Maya, and cards when the selected payment provider supports them. Payment availability, taxes, recurring billing behavior, and refunds must be represented accurately rather than assumed.

## Explicit non-goals

Zenote is not a complex multi-agent platform, workflow automation product, Notion replacement, RAG knowledge-management system, or enterprise collaboration suite. Do not expand into those areas unless explicitly instructed.
